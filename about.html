COMPANY OVERVIEW

Engineering Performance. Powering Institutions.

Captiv Techno Solutions Pvt. Ltd. was founded on a singular conviction: enterprises do not lack software implementations — they lack institutional systems that consistently perform under pressure.

OUR OPERATING PRINCIPLE

We stay until the system performs.

Most technology consultancies optimize for scope handoffs and billable hours. We optimize for institutional throughput, uptime, and latency.

Headquartered in Chennai, India, with engineering presences in the Middle East, Africa, and Southeast Asia, CAPTIV delivers precision-engineered digital infrastructure for organizations where downtime is not an inconvenience, but a systemic crisis.

From six of India’s busiest maritime ports to national security borders and pan-continental banking networks, our technology stack underpins operations that hundreds of millions of citizens rely on every single day.
