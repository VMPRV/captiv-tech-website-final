CASE STUDIES & DEPLOYMENTS

Engineering systems that actually perform.

Selected case studies documenting the real institutional challenges, custom architectures, and measurable business outcomes we deliver.

Maritime · Government of India

6 Major Port Enterprise Operating System

Coordinating port logistics, container terminal automation and customs digital integration across six of India's major sea ports.

The Challenge: Prior to deployment, vessel berthing, customs documentation and gate transactions were siloed across legacy disparate databases. Terminal turnaround delays compounded costs for global shipping lines and created inland logistics bottlenecks.

The Solution: CAPTIV engineered an enterprise business system unifying port operations, automated RFID gate verification, and EDI message exchanges. The platform processes high-concurrency customs declarations with sub-second response times.

Verified Operational Impact:
• 42% reduction in gate turnaround time across participating ports.
• Complete transition to paperless manifest processing saving millions of administrative hours.
• 99.98% platform uptime across 24/7/365 maritime operations.
Client: Indian Ports Association (IPA) Stack: Optima 360, QLens, Oracle ERP
Fintech · Pan-Africa

Pan-African Financial Operating Network

High-throughput core lending and multi-currency treasury platforms across 11 banks spanning 4 nations.

The Challenge: Operating cross-border financial services across Southern Africa requires navigating volatile forex rates, fragmented mobile money rails, and strict central banking compliance mandates in Zambia, Zimbabwe, Mozambique and Uganda.

The Solution: Deployed the AION financial operating system as a multi-tenant core platform. Integrated automated nostro/vostro reconciliation, sub-second credit decisioning, and AML sanctions filtering into a single pane of glass.

Verified Operational Impact:
• Automated loan origination turnaround compressed from 14 days to under 4 hours.
• Real-time multi-currency ledger balance verification preventing forex slippage.
• Scaled across 11 financial institutions with zero ledger discrepancies.
Clients: Lion Finance, Xtenda, FNB, BancABC Stack: AION Financial Engine, ISO 20022
AI Cloud · UAE

Sovereign GPU Cloud Infrastructure (UAE)

High-availability bare-metal GPU clusters engineered for national AI initiatives and enterprise LLM inference.

The Challenge: Global AI models require petabyte-scale datasets and enormous GPU clusters, but enterprise clients in the UAE demanded strict sovereign data residency within national borders, coupled with Tier-IV data center reliability.

The Solution: Engineered SkyVerse bare-metal NVIDIA H100 clusters in Dubai. Implemented automated FinOps cost governors, low-latency InfiniBand networking, and zero-trust hardware security modules (HSMs).

Verified Operational Impact:
• Sustained 99.999% compute availability during peak multi-node model training cycles.
• 40% improvement in GPU cluster utilization via automated queue orchestration.
• 100% regional compliance with UAE sovereign data residency standards.
Region: Dubai, UAE Stack: SkyVerse GPU Cluster, NVIDIA InfiniBand
