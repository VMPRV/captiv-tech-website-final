LEGAL COMPLIANCE & GOVERNANCE

Privacy Policy.

Last revised: January 1, 2026. This policy governs how CAPTIV Techno Solutions Pvt. Ltd. processes, protects, and respects institutional and personal data.

CAPTIV Techno Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("CAPTIV", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to upholding the highest standards of data integrity, confidentiality, and security. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collected through our websites, software platforms (SkyVerse, ProAug, Optima 360, AION, SonicLynk, QLens), and enterprise client engagements across India, the Middle East, Africa, and internationally.

1. Information We Receive & Process

We receive information depending on your interaction with our platforms:

  • Institutional & Technical Contacts: Name, work email address, professional phone number, institutional role, and organizational entity provided during inquiries, RFPs, or technical support requests.
  • Telemetry & Operational Logs: For managed enterprise deployments, our platforms record system performance telemetry, uptime metrics, API latency logs, and cryptographic audit hashes necessary to uphold service level agreements (SLAs).
  • Website Usage Data: Anonymized technical telemetry such as IP address ranges, browser user-agents, device categories, and page interaction timestamps collected to ensure cyber defense and site performance.

2. Legal Basis and Purpose of Processing

CAPTIV processes personal and institutional data strictly under legitimate legal bases recognized by applicable data protection frameworks, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (India), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the DIFC Data Protection Law (UAE):

  • Contractual Performance: To deliver, configure, and maintain mission-critical enterprise systems and platforms requested by our clients.
  • Legitimate Interests: To protect our sovereign network boundaries from cyber threats, unauthorized intrusion, and denial-of-service disruptions.
  • Regulatory & Legal Compliance: To satisfy statutory auditing, export control, anti-money laundering (AML), and central banking directives.

3. Data Security and Zero-Trust Isolation

CAPTIV enforces a rigorous Zero-Trust security model across all internal engineering systems and managed client deployments:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3 with forward secrecy.
  • All data at rest is encrypted using FIPS 140-2 certified AES-256 HSM cryptographic keys.
  • Client data repositories are logically and cryptographically partitioned with multi-tenant isolation, preventing cross-tenant leakage.
  • Access to production infrastructure requires hardware-backed multi-factor authentication (MFA) with strict role-based access controls (RBAC) and just-in-time privilege grants.

4. Data Retention & Sovereign Residency

We retain operational and personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purposes outlined in our enterprise contracts or as mandated by statutory retention requirements. For sovereign government and financial clients, data is strictly confined within designated geographical boundaries (e.g. data hosted within India or the UAE remains within respective sovereign borders).

5. Third-Party Disclosures

CAPTIV does not sell, lease, or monetize personal or institutional data. Data is shared only with vetted enterprise cloud providers (e.g., AWS, Azure, GCP, Oracle) under strict Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) or when compelled by lawful governmental court orders.

6. Data Subject Rights

Subject to applicable regional legislation, individuals have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information held by CAPTIV. To exercise any data subject rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@captivtech.com.
